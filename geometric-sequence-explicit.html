Geometric Sequences — Explicit Formula

Find the formula once, then jump straight to any term without listing all the ones before it.

an = a1 · rn − 1
  • A geometric sequence multiplies by the same number each time. That number is the common ratio r.
  • a₁ is the first term, n is the position of the term you want, and aₙ is that term.
  • To find r, divide any term by the term before it. Then the formula lets you jump to any term directly.

Change the first term, the ratio, and which term you want. The formula fills in with your numbers, and the term aₙ is found without listing every term in between. In practice mode you build it yourself, step by step.
